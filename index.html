Hey! I’m Kaushik. People call me 🐮.

I design digital products that solve problems and hopefully make people go, “okay, that was actually nice to use.”

I started out designing posters and visual creatives in college, mostly because I liked making things look cool. Somewhere along the way, I realised I was way more interested in why things worked (or didn’t). That curiosity pulled me into product design, and I’ve been happily obsessing over it ever since.

Over the last few years, I’ve worked across B2C and SaaS, building products that help businesses and their users get along better. Lately, I’ve been deep in AI-powered features, trying to make complex tech feel less intimidating and more… human.

When I’m not designing, I’m behind a camera, rage-queuing Valorant, watching anime, or randomly obsessing over finance, watches and sneakers.

Got a project in mind? Let’s talk about bringing it to life.
