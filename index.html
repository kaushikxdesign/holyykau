About Me

Hey! I'm a Senior Product Designer. People call me 🐮.

I started out designing posters in college because I liked making things look cool. Somewhere along the way I realised I cared more about why things worked, or didn't, and that pulled me into product design.

Right now I'm designing at Multiplier. Before that I spent four years at Freshworks making AI-powered support tools feel less intimidating and more human.

Off the clock: behind a camera, rage-queuing Valorant, watching anime, or obsessing over finance, watches and sneakers.
